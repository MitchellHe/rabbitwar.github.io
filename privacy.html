Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Email Calendar Automation is a personal productivity application that helps identify calendar events from email messages and create corresponding Google Calendar events.

Information We Process

The application may process email message information, including message subjects, snippets, dates, and event details, to determine whether a calendar event should be created.

How Information Is Used

Email information is processed to identify potential events. When an event is identified, the relevant event details may be sent to Google Calendar to create a calendar entry.

Data Storage and Processing

The application is designed to run on the user's own computer. Email analysis is performed using a locally hosted language model. The workflow may retain message identifiers to avoid processing the same email more than once.

Third-Party Services

The application integrates with Google Gmail and Google Calendar. Information accessed through these services is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally sell personal information or share email content for advertising purposes.

Security

Users are responsible for securing their own computer, application credentials, and connected Google accounts.
